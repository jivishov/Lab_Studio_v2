**What the runtime depends on** (a refinement must not move it without a decision):
- real dimensions, which never change (`AGENTS.md`);
- the fill profiles: liquid levels, graduations and the drop-to-the-line tolerance. The dropping pipette is
  155 mm long (its fill profile reaches 154.4 mm) and the 25 mL cylinder's rim is 150 mm high. The tip cannot
  reach a 20 mL level in the 100 mL cylinder, which is why the sample is measured in the 25 mL one (D8 (9));
- anchors, keyed by the zone ids in `src/domain/interactionZones.ts`;
- footprints (the registry's `footprintMm`): the size of the beacon and selection rings, and placement;
- the mesh itself: picking raycasts the meshes (`BenchEngine.pick`), so a shape change moves what a click
  hits; an item under 10 mm tall gets an invisible box to pick instead;
- bounding boxes, which `BenchEngine` reads at run time: a pipette held over a vessel is posed from the
  vessel's top (`poseHeld`); the screen targets (`screenTargets`) are centred on them; the inversion and the
  swirl take an item's height;
- material names: the viewer assigns materials by name, and the validator refuses any not in
  `src/studio3d/equipment3d/viewerMaterials.json`, so a new material is also a change under `src/`.
Bevels, markings, surface detail and tessellation are free to improve, within the budgets and the fidelity
rules.
